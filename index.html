Methodology & limitations

The way results are produced matters more than the results.

How every number here was generated

All results come from TradingView's Strategy Tester on daily bars: 0.05% commission per side 1 tick slippage fills on bar close $10,000 initial capital. No intrabar assumptions, no look-ahead constructs (entries reference prior-bar levels), and inputs were left at their designed defaults for all cross-symbol tests — the MSFT and SPY numbers below were produced with parameters frozen exactly as tuned on the primary symbol.

Design discipline

Each system keeps its tunable surface deliberately small (5–10 inputs), and each page documents rejected experiments with their measured numbers — faster rebalancing that destroyed returns, trailing exits that degraded a reversion edge, position sizing that produced an 89% drawdown. A strategy is what survives its own falsification attempts.

What these backtests are not

These are in-sample, single-history results. They have not yet been through walk-forward splits, parameter-perturbation Monte Carlo, or live paper verification — that pipeline (which I run on my intraday NQ futures research: code backtest, blind replay, live paper, independent reconstruction, and TradingView cross-check as five separate validation layers) is the planned next stage for this library. Until then, treat every figure as an upper bound on expectancy, not a forecast. Past performance does not predict future results.
